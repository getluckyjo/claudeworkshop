Subject: Saturday — thank you

Morning all,

Thank you for Saturday. Every one of you went the whole way through, and
every one of you left with something real running. That's not how these
usually go.

There is always something exhilarating about getting entrepreneurs in a
room together. We are a special breed, and you could feel it on Saturday.

But the builds weren't the best part. The best part was watching the room
work out how much of what you do every week doesn't actually need you to
do it. Once you've seen that you can't unsee it, and that's worth more
than anything we made on the day.

Asset pack attached. The prompt library, a Project template to fill in,
the cheat sheet, and the workshop skill. Your own skill isn't in there on
purpose — everyone's came out different because you wrote them off
whatever had just worked, and they live on your laptop rather than mine.
Page 4 is how to rebuild or extend it if you need to.

The 14-day call is Friday 4 September, 10:00, online. One win and one
blocker each. Come with something you actually tried, even if it broke —
especially if it broke. That's the call that decides whether this sticks.
The written 30-day review follows after it.

One ask between now and then: do one thing. Not three. The people this
works for are the ones who used it on the Monday, not the ones with the
longest list.

And if you build something good, send it to me. Nothing sells the next
cohort better.
