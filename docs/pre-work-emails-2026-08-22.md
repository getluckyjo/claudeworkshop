## 1. Danielle — d distillery

**Subject:** Before Saturday — 20 minutes of setup

Danielle,

Good to have you in the room on Saturday. 10:00 at Ideas Cartel, Claremont, and
we finish at 13:00.

Three things before then. The first two are quick, the third is the one that
makes the difference.

**1. Get Claude working properly — 10 minutes**

You need a paid plan (USD 20/month, so around R400 depending on the rand and
your bank's forex margin — billed by Anthropic, not by me). Then install the
desktop app, not just the browser version.

If your card declines, it's almost always the bank blocking an international
online payment rather than anything wrong on Anthropic's side. Enable
international purchases in your banking app and try again. Shout if it's still
fighting you — I'd rather sort it on Wednesday than at 10:05 on Saturday.

**2. Add the workshop skill — 2 minutes**

This is what runs the session with you, so it needs to be in before you arrive:

Download it here: https://github.com/getluckyjo/claudeworkshop/raw/HEAD/dist/entrepreneurcoach.zip

**Don't unzip it.** If your Mac unzips it automatically and you end up with a
folder, ignore the folder — the `.zip` is still sitting in Downloads next to it.

Then in Claude: click your name bottom-left → **Settings** → **Customize** →
find **Skills** → upload the zip. You'll know it worked when **Entrepreneur
Coach** shows up in your list.

**3. Claude 101 — about an hour, by Wednesday**

Anthropic's own free course. This is what buys us the right to skip the basics
and spend the three hours on your actual business instead.

**On the day, bring:** a laptop, a charger, and one real, messy file from the
business. Messy is the point — a tidy example teaches you nothing. Your logo and
a couple of photos too, if they're handy.

**And one question:** are you on Gmail or Outlook? One word back is fine.

---

You named five models: demand forecasting by SKU, procurement against lead times
and MOQs, costing by product and channel, project profitability, and the rolling
cash-flow forecast.

We're building one of them on Saturday — the **project profitability model**. The
go/no-go on a production run. Costs in, price in, terms in, and out comes what
you need to charge, how much you need to sell, and how fast.

I've picked that one deliberately. It's the one with a decision attached, it
doesn't need a data pipeline to work, and once it exists the other four have
somewhere to plug into. Trying to build all five in an afternoon gets you five
half-models and no decisions.

So bring:

- Your real cost lines for **one** product — inputs, production, packaging
- What you actually sell it for, by channel
- Payment terms both ways, and what it costs you to borrow
- **One production run you're weighing up right now.** We'll model that one, live

Are you on Xero? If yes, we'll point Claude at it on the day.

See you Saturday.

Johannes
