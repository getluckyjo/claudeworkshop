# WhatsApp messages for the cohort

Copy-paste ready. WhatsApp formatting: `*bold*`, `_italic_`.

**Before you send:** shorten the download link. The raw GitHub URL is long and
looks sketchy to a beginner on a phone. Any shortener works — just make sure the
short link still resolves to the `.zip`.

---

## Notes

- **"Do this on your laptop, not your phone"** is the most important line in
  message 1. People read WhatsApp on their phones, and downloading a zip then
  uploading it to Claude's settings on mobile is miserable. Say it early, say it
  in bold.
- Message 1 deliberately gives them permission to fail. Beginners who arrive
  feeling behind stay quiet all session, and quiet people don't ask the questions
  that make a workshop work.
- Keep "no coding, nothing to install" in. For this audience it's the sentence
  that gets the message read to the end.
